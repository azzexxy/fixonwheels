    Before you book

    Where does the repair happen?

    In our van, parked at your address. It's a small workshop with a bench, tools and parts for the most common phones. You can sit in and watch, or go back inside and we'll ring the doorbell when it's done.

    Do I need to give you my passcode?

    No. For a screen or battery swap we don't need it. At the end you unlock the phone yourself so we can test the touch screen, camera and charging together.

    My phone is insured. Can you help?

    Yes. You get a full invoice with the repair details, which you can send to your insurer. If your insurer has to approve the repair first, book once you have their OK.

    It's a company phone.

    We can put the invoice in your employer's name. If your company has several people with broken phones, we can come to the office during the day. Get in touch.

    What if you can't fix it in the van?

    Some laptop repairs or rare parts need more time. In that case we tell you on the spot and you decide: we order the part and come back, or you keep the device and pay nothing.
